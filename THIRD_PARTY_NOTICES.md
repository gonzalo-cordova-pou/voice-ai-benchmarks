## Semantic Scholar

Citation counts and paper identifiers in `data/benchmark-stats.json` are retrieved from the [Semantic Scholar Academic Graph API](https://www.semanticscholar.org/product/api). Each measurement includes its source, source URL, and retrieval date; the website labels citation counts as Semantic Scholar data.

Use of the API and its data is subject to [Semantic Scholar's API license agreement](https://www.semanticscholar.org/product/api/license) and applicable data licenses. [Additional API-hosted terms](https://api.semanticscholar.org/license/) are also published by the provider. These provider terms are not an MIT grant from this project. Check the applicable terms with the provider before commercial reuse or redistributing the collected data as a separate dataset.
